Our Programs

Pathways to Potential

Every young person is more than a student, more than a future employee. Our five connected pathways invest in the whole person — helping stop problems before they start, and walking alongside those already facing life's challenges.

01
Pathway One

Mentorship & Leadership Development

To nurture confident, ethical, resilient and purpose-driven young people through meaningful mentoring relationships and leadership experiences.

What We Do

Youth mentorship forums School mentorship programmes Leadership academies Holiday mentorship camps Peer mentorship clubs Young Leaders Fellowship Volunteer & community service

What Success Looks Like

Young people who are equipped to thrive, supported when challenges arise and able to give back to their families and communities.
02
Pathway Two

Education & Career Development

To equip young people with the knowledge, guidance and opportunities needed to succeed in education, discover meaningful careers and pursue lifelong learning.

What We Do

Career guidance & mentorship Academic support STEM promotion Scholarship & bursary awareness Vocational training & university transition Digital literacy Reading & innovation clubs

What Success Looks Like

Young people who stay in school, move on successfully into further education or a job, and confidently pursue their goals.
03
Pathway Three

Health & Wellbeing

To promote the physical, emotional, mental and social wellbeing of young people by equipping them with knowledge, resilience and healthy decision-making skills.

What We Do

Mental health awareness Emotional & social support Sexual & reproductive health education Menstrual health education Nutrition & healthy lifestyles Sports & recreation Substance abuse prevention Family support

What Success Looks Like

Young people who are equipped to thrive, supported when challenges arise, and able to give back to their families and communities.
04
Pathway Four

Economic Empowerment

To help young people earn a stable living by building business, money and job skills.

What We Do

Starting a business Money management Saving & investing Digital & job skills Farming & eco-friendly businesses Business support Job readiness

What Success Looks Like

Young people who create opportunities for themselves, help their local economy grow and become more financially independent.
05
Pathway Five

Community & Family Engagement

To strengthen the support around every young person by bringing families, schools, community leaders and institutions together as partners in raising strong young people.

What We Do

Parent & guardian forums School partnerships Community dialogues Working with faith & cultural leaders Volunteer recruitment Speaking up for young people Community awareness events

What Success Looks Like

Communities where young people are supported by strong relationships, shared responsibility and a strong spirit of mentorship and opportunity.
Our Main Journey

The Purpose Project

While each Pathway to Potential covers an important part of youth development, real, lasting change happens when these experiences come together on purpose. Rather than asking young people only what they want to become, The Purpose Project begins with a deeper question: Who are you becoming?

“Who am I?”

Discover

Young people explore their identity, values, strengths, gifts and aspirations, recognising that their circumstances do not define their future.

“What can I become?”

Develop

Participants build life skills, leadership skills, emotional intelligence, communication skills and healthy decision-making habits.

“How can I serve others?”

Lead

Young people move beyond personal growth to community leadership — leading by serving others and mentoring their peers.

“How can I leave my community better than I found it?”

Impact

Participants apply their knowledge, skills and values to create lasting change through entrepreneurship, new ideas and community service.

No Young Person Walks Alone

Whatever a young person's starting point, we walk alongside them with compassion, mentorship, practical support and hope. Help us reach more of them.
