A Vision for Youth Development

Empowering Young People. Strengthening Communities.

The Matele Foundation exists so that every young person in Sachangwan discovers purpose, develops their potential and pursues a future filled with hope — through mentorship, education, leadership and community that believes in them.

Our Founding Belief

Turning Belief into Meaningful Impact

We do not define young people by the challenges they face. We define them by the possibilities they carry. We believe that lasting change does not come from waiting for a crisis — it comes from investing early, walking alongside young people, strengthening families and creating places where every child can discover purpose, build confidence and give back to their community.

  • We see potential before problems
  • We invest in people before programmes
  • We believe communities are built together

Our Programs

Five Pathways to Potential

Investing in the whole person — five connected areas of youth development that guide every programme we run.

Mentorship & Leadership

Nurturing confident, ethical, purpose-driven young people through meaningful mentoring and leadership experiences.

Education & Career

Equipping young people to succeed in education, discover meaningful careers and pursue lifelong learning.

Health & Wellbeing

Promoting physical, emotional, mental and social wellbeing through knowledge, resilience and healthy decisions.

Economic Empowerment

Building business, money and job skills so young people can earn a steady, secure living.

Community & Family

Strengthening the network of support around every young person — families, schools and community leaders.
