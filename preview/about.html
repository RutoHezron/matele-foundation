About Us

A Community Choosing to Believe in Its Young People

Matele Foundation was born from a simple but powerful belief: that every young person deserves the opportunity to discover their purpose, develop their potential and pursue a future filled with hope.

Our Story

The Dream Behind the Foundation

Every community has dreams — of children who will one day become teachers, engineers, nurses, entrepreneurs, artists, farmers, leaders and changemakers. Sachangwan is no different: rich in resilience, culture and untapped potential, home to generations of young people whose aspirations are as vast as their possibilities.

Yet many of those possibilities are interrupted long before they have the chance to become reality. Poverty, school dropout, limited access to mentorship, unemployment, substance abuse, early marriage and teenage pregnancy continue to shape the lives of many children and young people — not because they lack ability, but because they often lack the support systems needed to thrive.

The Foundation began with conversations among young people who had grown up in Sachangwan — conversations about the community they loved, the challenges they had witnessed and the future they believed was still possible. Among those voices was Keith Matele, a passionate believer in the power of community, leadership and young people. He envisioned a future where mentorship would become a bridge between generations, where knowledge would replace misinformation and where every young person would know that their future mattered.

Although Matele did not live to see that vision fully realised, his dream did not end with him. It became a shared responsibility. Matele Foundation was established to carry that vision forward — not simply in his memory, but through meaningful action that transforms lives and strengthens communities.

“This is more than the story of an organisation. It is the story of a community choosing to believe in its young people.”
— Matele Foundation
Founding Principles

What Guides Everything We Do

We See Potential Before Problems

Every young person carries unique gifts, talents and possibilities. Our work begins not with what is wrong, but with what is possible.

We Invest in People Before Programmes

Real change happens through genuine relationships, mentorship, education and shared experiences.

We Believe Communities Are Built Together

Families, schools, faith communities, government, volunteers and young people all have a vital role to play.

We Lead with Integrity and Compassion

Trust is earned through honesty, accountability, transparency and respect for every individual's dignity.

We Measure Success by Lives Transformed

Not by the number of activities we conduct, but by the young person who discovers purpose, the student who remains in school, and the community that becomes more hopeful because people chose to invest in one another.

Our Vision

A Thriving Community

A thriving community where every young person is empowered to realise their full potential and help create positive change.

Our Mission

Empowering Through Opportunity

To empower children, adolescents and young people through mentorship, education, leadership development, life skills, community involvement and strong partnerships that create opportunities, build resilience and transform communities.

“When young people have purpose, opportunity, guidance and hope, communities flourish.”

Our Core Values

Safeguarding, Protection & Inclusion

Every Young Person Deserves Dignity

At Matele Foundation, we believe that every child and young person has the right to grow up in a safe, supportive and nurturing environment where they are treated with dignity, respect and compassion. Safeguarding is not simply a policy — it is a responsibility shared by every board member, staff member, volunteer, mentor and partner associated with the Foundation.

We recognise that some young people come to us already facing significant challenges. Our response is never one of judgement or exclusion. Instead, we walk alongside every young person with compassion, practical support and appropriate referrals to trained professionals — because every individual deserves dignity, hope and the opportunity to rebuild their future.
